Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your PupSpot account and saved progress.

Option 1: in the app (recommended)

  1. Open PupSpot and tap the gear icon on the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Continue, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

This erases your cloud save and leaderboard entry, removes your sign-in account, and signs you out.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email onthegogames.dev@gmail.com.
  2. Send the email from the Google account address you signed in to PupSpot with.
  3. Use the subject line PupSpot account deletion.

We reply to confirm the request, then delete your data within 30 days of confirming. We confirm by replying to the Google account address, so only the account owner can request deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your cloud save: pups, coins, levels, sniffs, crates, quests, XP, and stats.
  • Your leaderboard entry.
  • Your sign-in account with Firebase Authentication.

What does not

  • Google Play order history. Your sniff pack purchases stay in your Google Play records. Refund requests go to Google Play.
  • Progress only on your device. If you never signed in, use Reset all progress in Settings, or uninstall the app.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Sniffs are removed with the account and cannot be restored.
