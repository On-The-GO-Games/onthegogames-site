Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

On The GO Games (“we”, “us”) makes quick, on-the-go games. This policy covers our website at onthegogames.is-a.dev and the apps we publish. It starts with PupSpot, available on Google Play. When a new game needs something different, it will get its own section here.

1. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. The site uses no cookies, no analytics, and no ads. It loads the Fredoka and Nunito fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google’s privacy policy.

2. PupSpot: what we collect

  • Account details, only if you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and Google account ID. Sign-in is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication. We never see your Google password.
  • Game progress, only if you are signed in: levels, pups, coins, sniffs, crates, daily quest progress, XP, stats, and your profile name. This is stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Leaderboard: your profile name, XP, level, and number of levels cleared. Other signed-in players can see these four things.
  • Purchases: when you buy a sniff pack, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a confirmation (product ID, purchase token, and order ID) so we can add the sniffs to your account. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • On your device: sound, music, and vibration settings, and game progress if you are not signed in. This stays on the device until you reset progress or uninstall the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID. PupSpot has no ads and no analytics.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and save your progress.
  • To show the leaderboard.
  • To add sniffs after a purchase.
  • To keep the games working and secure.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

We use Google services to run our apps: Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (sign-in and saved data), and Google Play Billing (purchases). They process data for us under their own terms. See Google’s privacy policy and Firebase’s privacy information.

5. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping your data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database rules let each player read and change only their own saved progress. The leaderboard only exposes the four public fields listed above.

7. How long we keep data, and how to delete it

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete it in either of two ways:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This erases your cloud save, your leaderboard entry, and your sign-in account, and signs you out.
  • On the web: follow the steps at onthegogames.is-a.dev/delete-account.html. You do not need to install or open the app.

Deletion is permanent. Purchases stay in your Google Play order history. The sniffs are removed from the game, and we cannot refund them through the game. Copies in Google’s backups, if any, are removed under Google’s own retention schedule.

8. Your choices

  • Sign out in Settings to stop syncing. Your cloud save stays until you delete it.
  • Use Reset all progress in Settings to clear the game data on this device. Sniffs are kept.
  • Delete your account as described in section 7.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also show a notice in the app.
